# Component Selection

## Solar PV

Four 20 W panels are documented, giving an installed 80 W PV capacity.

## Wind generators

Low-KV BLDC/gimbal motors are proposed as experimental permanent-magnet generators because they are commercially accessible and can be mechanically driven.

Their actual generator output must be measured rather than inferred from motor ratings.

## AeroLeaf

Thin aluminium is proposed because it is lightweight, corrosion-resistant and relatively easy to fabricate and modify.

## ESP32

ESP32 provides:
- processing
- Wi-Fi
- sensor interfacing
- telemetry
- dashboard connectivity

## Mechanical structure

The tree frame is intended to be modular, locally fabricable and serviceable.

## Final component selection rule

Before procurement, verify:
- electrical voltage rating
- current rating
- environmental rating
- mechanical loading
- connector compatibility
- safety margin
- actual supplier datasheet
