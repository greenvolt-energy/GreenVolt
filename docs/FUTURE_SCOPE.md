# Future Scope

The project documentation identifies the following future directions:

1. CFD-based AeroLeaf rotor optimization
2. Improved power coefficient and low-speed performance
3. Forecast-aware wind/solar energy management
4. Weather and irradiance forecasting
5. Energy scheduling
6. Multiple modular GreenVolt nodes
7. Higher aggregate generation
8. Long-term outdoor field testing
9. Real energy-yield analysis
10. Reliability and environmental durability analysis
11. LiFePO4 battery with advanced BMS
12. AI-assisted predictive load forecasting
13. Automated dynamic load shifting
14. Larger public-space and smart-city deployment

These are future/planned developments, not current measured capabilities unless separately demonstrated.
