# Technical Approach

## VAWT

The AeroLeaf is based on a Savonius-inspired vertical-axis configuration.

Design considerations:
- curved blade profile
- controlled central overlap
- aerodynamic air passages
- reinforcement ribs
- low-speed starting torque
- rotor balance
- blade spacing
- rotor height-to-diameter ratio

Documented prototype dimensions:
- approximately 800 mm height
- approximately 423 mm maximum profile width
- approximately 76 mm central overlap

These dimensions are design/prototype values and require experimental validation.

## Generator

The project proposes up to eight low-KV BLDC/gimbal motors used as permanent-magnet generator units.

Actual electrical output must be measured experimentally.

## Solar

The documented prototype uses:
- 4 × 20 W PV panels
- total installed PV capacity: 80 W
- modular branch mounting

## Materials

AeroLeaf prototype:
- thin aluminium sheet
- approximately 1–2 mm depending on final design
- ribs/edge reinforcement as required
- protective finishing

Production alternatives may include FRP/GFRP or carbon-fiber structures where justified.
