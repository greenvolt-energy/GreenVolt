# Braking, Protection and Safety

## Electrical protection

Planned/proposed protection includes:
- fuses
- DC breakers/MCBs where appropriate
- DC isolators
- reverse-polarity protection
- blocking diodes where required
- emergency-stop switch
- appropriate grounding/earthing
- outdoor-rated enclosure

## Wind protection

The design includes an electrical braking / dump-load provision and a mechanical protection concept.

The exact implementation must be documented with:
- circuit diagram
- controller specification
- trigger threshold
- braking load rating
- mechanical protection details
- test evidence

## High-wind condition

The source document identifies high wind and over-speed as a risk.

Before field operation, define and verify:
- maximum safe rotor RPM
- controller threshold
- braking response
- dump-load capacity
- emergency shutdown behavior

## Safety warning

Do not operate a rotating prototype outdoors without suitable guarding, secure mounting, electrical isolation and emergency shutdown.
