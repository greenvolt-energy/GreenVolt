# Feasibility, Risks and Mitigation

| Challenge | Impact | Proposed mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Low/variable wind | Low wind-energy capture | Hybrid Savonius-inspired rotor and experimental optimization |
| Generator cogging | Difficult starting | Low-cogging generator selection |
| Battery saturation | Limits regenerative energy absorption | Dump-load/electrical braking and mechanical protection |
| Outdoor environment | Heat, humidity, corrosion | Appropriate materials, coating and IP-rated enclosure |
| Dual-source control | Coordination complexity | Independent source control loops |
| Faulty branch | Could affect common bus | Fuses, isolation and modular branch architecture |
| Rotor overspeed | Mechanical/electrical risk | Braking and emergency shutdown |

## Serviceability

The design proposes detachable branches and modular mechanical/electrical connections so individual modules can be serviced without dismantling the entire tree.
