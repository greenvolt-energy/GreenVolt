# Project Overview

## Name

**GreenVolt — Dual Mode Tree**

## Concept

A tree-inspired hybrid renewable-energy platform combining:
1. Solar PV generation
2. AeroLeaf-inspired vertical-axis wind generation
3. Common power management
4. Battery storage
5. IoT monitoring
6. Electrical protection
7. Modular serviceability

## Objectives

- Develop a hybrid solar-wind renewable-energy system.
- Integrate approximately 80 W of PV capacity in the documented prototype plan.
- Develop and optimize an AeroLeaf-inspired VAWT.
- Investigate a multi-generator wind architecture.
- Integrate renewable sources through common power management.
- Monitor voltage, current, power, RPM and battery status.
- Develop the prototype within approximately ₹50,000.
- Provide a modular platform for small DC loads, IoT devices and lighting.
- Experimentally evaluate solar generation, wind generation, mechanical losses, conversion efficiency and hybrid output.

## Important limitation

The wind-generator output is not assumed from the motor's rated motor power. Actual generator performance must be determined through RPM, torque, load and electrical measurements.
