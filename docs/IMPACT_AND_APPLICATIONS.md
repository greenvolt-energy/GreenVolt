# Impact and Applications

## Environmental

- Renewable electricity from solar and wind
- Combined use of two renewable sources
- Compact tree-inspired architecture
- Potential contribution to sustainable urban infrastructure

## Energy

- Solar generation during daylight
- Wind generation when wind is available
- Battery storage
- Short-term buffering through a supercapacitor where implemented
- Source-specific power management

## Potential applications

Subject to measured available power:
- IoT devices
- small DC loads
- LED lighting
- educational campuses
- parks
- public spaces
- charging demonstrations

## Maintenance

IoT monitoring can provide visibility into:
- voltage
- current
- power
- RPM
- battery status
- environmental parameters

## Important qualification

Applications and loads must be selected based on actual measured energy availability. The prototype should not be represented as capable of powering large loads without supporting measurements.
